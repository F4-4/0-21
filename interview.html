PM Interview Notes

Private notes to refresh main talking points and prepare for interviews.

What an interviewer is measuring

  • Fit & Credibility

    Do you understand their specific problem(s), and can you back up your background with real verifiable depth?

  • Execution & Proof

    Can you communicate clearly, drive hard metrics, and get into the weeds of product build and analytics?

  • Strategy & Decision-Making

    How do you evaluate trade-offs, weigh risks, and tie product choices directly to business revenue and market positioning?

  • Leadership & Adaptability

    How do you bring people with you, resolve conflict, course correct, and learn from mistakes?

Key Themes

Discovery

  • How do you approach discovery? Tell me about a time you validated a user problem and how you identified it?

Discovery

  • Framing: Define the core user pain point and business impact before touching solutions.
  • Insights: Combine quant (funnels, cohort data) with qual (user interviews, support tickets).
  • De-risking: Test assumptions early via low-fi prototypes, landing page tests, or concierge MVPs.
  • Alignment: Partner with Design & Eng to evaluate value, usability, feasibility, and viability.

Diagnosing

  • How do you diagnose what went wrong? Walk me through your step-by-step diagnostic process for a problem you faced.

Diagnosing

  • Framing: Define the core user pain point and business impact before touching solutions.
  • Insights: Combine quant (funnels, cohort data) with qual (user interviews, support tickets).
  • De-risking: Test assumptions early via low-fi prototypes, landing page tests, or concierge MVPs.
  • Alignment: Partner with Design & Eng to evaluate value, usability, feasibility, and viability.

Evaluating

  • How do you measure success? Tell me about a time you evaluated a feature's performance and what actions you took based on the results.

Evaluating

  • Success Metrics: Track adoption, retention, and North Star impact against baseline goals.
  • Cohort Analysis: Evaluate user segment behavior over time to verify sustained value.
  • Feedback Loops: Gather post-launch qualitative feedback to capture unexpected edge cases.
  • Iteration: Decide whether to double down, pivot, or sunset based on actual ROI.

Prioritising

  • How do you arrange priorities? Tell me about a tradeoff you have made under pressure and roadmap planning.

Prioritising

  • Structure: Apply scoring frameworks to evaluate objectively (RICE, Value vs. Effort).
  • Alignment: Map every initiative back to top-level business goals and North Star metrics.
  • Tradeoffs: Balance user value, tech debt, speed-to-market and revenue impacts.
  • Pushback: Say "no" or "later" with clear rationale, alternatives and evidence.

Influencing

  • How do you influence stakeholders? Tell me about a time you had to convince someone and how you handled it.

Influencing

  • Empathy: What does engineering, design, legal, marketing care about? (tech debt, compliance...).
  • Data: Remove personal opinion, bring evidence (research, analytics, user feedback...).
  • Alignment: How does it map to company goals, OKRs, outcomes?
  • Trade-offs: Willing to compromise (scope / timing) to achieve results and maintain relationships.

Reflecting

  • How do you reflect on work? Tell me about a failure or mistake you learned from, and what you'd do differently looking back.

Reflecting

  • Blameless Reviews: Focus on systemic causes and process fixes rather than pointing fingers.
  • Data vs. Hypotheses: Dissect gaps between expected outcomes and actual user behavior.
  • Feedback: Solicit structured feedback from eng, design, and users (e.g., retro cadences).
  • Awareness: Demonstrate humility, ownership, and immediate iteration.

Conflicts

  • How do you handle conflict? Tell me about a time you had a disagreement or received pushback, and handled conflicting priorities.

Conflicts

  • Empathy: uncover the underlying incentives and core concerns behind the pushback.
  • Objective Framing: shift debates from personal opinions to shared metrics, data, and user value.
  • Collaborative Trade-offs: use frameworks (e.g. RICE, trade-off matrix) to negotiate scope vs. speed.
  • Disagree and Commit: align on a path forward with clear ownership, even if full consensus isn't hit.

Emergency

  • How do you handle emergencies? Tell me about an incident you managed under pressure.

Emergency

  • Rapid Triage & Containment stop the bleeding first (e.g., feature flag, rollback) before debugging.
  • Incident Command & Comms establish a single source of truth, run status syncs, and manage stakeholders.
  • Customer First focus on blast radius, user impact, and transparent external communication.
  • Post-Incident Prevention run a blameless retro to build systemic guardrails and prevent recurrence.
